Bhartiya Gramodhan Sanstha Hosts Inspiring Career Guidance and Mentorship Session

Rishikesh: In a dedicated effort to empower the children of artisans associated with the organization, Bhartiya Gramodhan Sanstha (BGS) organized a motivational dialogue session aimed at fostering academic excellence and personal growth. The event focused on providing students with comprehensive guidance on education, career pathing, time management, strategic exam preparation, and effective success strategies.

Get More Info

The session was graced by the presence of Ms. Ishita Singh, the Rishikesh City Topper in the CBSE Class 12th Board examinations, and Dr. Sunita Sharma, Principal of Nirmal Ashram Gyan Daan Academy (NGA).

Celebrating Resilience and Excellence

Ms. Ishita Singh, who achieved an impressive 98.4% in her board examinations, has become a beacon of inspiration for students across the region. As the daughter of an auto-rickshaw driver, her journey to the top, marked by unwavering determination and hard work in the face of challenges, serves as a powerful testament to what can be achieved through perseverance. Bhartiya Gramodhan Sanstha, which currently operates a free computer training center and a library for artisans' children, organized this dialogue to encourage these students to pursue their academic goals with confidence.




An Interactive Dialogue on Success

Conducted in a talk-show format, the session offered students a unique opportunity to engage directly with the City Topper. Students posed insightful questions regarding focus, study techniques, goal setting, and career development. Sharing her personal journey, Ms. Ishita emphasized that success is rooted in discipline, consistency, self-confidence, and persistent effort. She urged her fellow students to value the guidance of their teachers and remain steadfast in their commitment to their goals, regardless of circumstances.

Expert Insights and Institutional Support : Dr. Sunita Sharma, Principal of NGA, addressed the gathering by highlighting the pivotal role of education, positive mindset, and continuous learning. She encouraged students to dedicate themselves to their dreams with grit and integrity.

Ms. Gita Chandola, Chairperson of Bhartiya Gramodhan Sanstha, commended Ishita’s remarkable achievement, labeling it as a sterling example of diligence and passion. During the event, the organization officially felicitated Ms. Singh, presenting her with a certificate of honor and a scholarship grant to support her future endeavors. The event also featured interactive sessions with esteemed guests, including NRI philanthropists Dinesh and Seema Goyal, who inspired the students to pursue their ambitions with confidence. Furthermore, Mr. Dinesh Painyuli from NGA spoke on the importance of sports and physical activity in achieving a balanced, holistic personality development.

Successful Coordination and Future Vision

The success of the event was bolstered by the contributions of the BGS team, including Public Relations Officer Mr. N.P. Kuksal, Radio Rishikesh Station Head Ms. Raksha Upadhyay, and Technical Head Mr. Ankit Rawat, who managed the operations and coordination. The event concluded with a vote of thanks to all guests and participants. The students expressed that the session was highly enlightening, providing them with essential tools for their academic and professional journeys. Bhartiya Gramodhan Sanstha reaffirmed its commitment to organizing similar mentorship initiatives in the future to nurture the potential of local youth.




"United Efforts, Shared Progress: The Spirit of BGS."

"At the heart of every initiative led by Bhartiya Gramodhan Sanstha lies the power of unity. Through our recent series of activities, we have witnessed firsthand what is possible when dedicated effort meets community passion. Together with the people we serve, we are not just completing projects; we are building a foundation of self-reliance, empowerment, and lasting progress for our region."

Our efforts and achievements stand as a testament to our continuous commitment to sustainable impact.”

Several projects were organized to promote the diversification of our natural products, creating new opportunities for innovation and income generation. This initiative not only provided employment to local artisans but also aimed at offering the rural communities of Uttarakhand a sustainable and reliable livelihood option, thereby contributing to both economic empowerment and eco-friendly development.
